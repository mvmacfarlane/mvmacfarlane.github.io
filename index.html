Welcome!

I am a Research Scientist at Lila Sciences, previously a PhD candidate at the University of Amsterdam. My research focuses on Open Ended Learning, Reinforcement Learning and Search.

My work includes Sequential Monte Carlo Policy Optimisation, meta-learning search processes, Searching Latent Program Spaces, and learning latent spaces in Reinforcement Learning. I'm always happy to discuss these topics!


About me

I hold a Master's in Artificial Intelligence from the University of St Andrews and a Bachelor's in Mathematics with Economics from the London School of Economics. I've worked as a Research Scientist at Lila Sciences and am a PhD candidate at the University of Amsterdam under Dr. Herke van Hoof.

I have industry experience through internships at Microsoft, InstaDeep, and Techspert. My research has been published at top-tier conferences including NeurIPS and ICML. I also have experience in asset management as a quant at Aberdeen Standard Investments.
